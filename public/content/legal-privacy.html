We do NOT sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except in the following circumstances:

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Credit report data is retained for 90 days after account deletion. Aggregated anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)

  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Secure Cloudflare infrastructure with DDoS protection
  • JWT-based authentication with RS256 signing

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following data protection rights:

  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
