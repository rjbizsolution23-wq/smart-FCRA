Core Terms

SecuritySeries Seed Preferred Stock, convertible into Common Stock at the option of the holder and automatically upon a Qualified Financing.
Round SizeAggregate $500,000 raised from lead + syndicate investors, with option to expand to $650,000 for strategic operators.
ValuationPre-money: $2,800,000 · Post-money: $3,300,000 (fully diluted, including 12% option pool refresh).
Price Per Share$0.35 per share (based on 8,000,000 fully-diluted shares outstanding pre-round).
Option PoolCompany will maintain a 12% fully-diluted employee option pool, refreshed pre-money as needed.
DividendsNon-cumulative; when-and-if declared by Board, pro-rata basis to preferred + common.
Liquidation Preference1× non-participating preferred, then participating with common on as-converted basis.
Anti-DilutionBroad-based weighted average anti-dilution protection for down rounds.
VotingPreferred votes on as-converted basis with common. Protective provisions on: sale of company, new class of stock, incurring debt > $250K, changes to Bylaws / Certificate.
Board3-member board: 2 Founder (Rick Jefferson) · 1 Investor Director (elected by Series Seed).
Information RightsQuarterly financials + annual audited (once revenue > $2M). Investor observer right at Board meetings.
Pro Rata RightsMajor Investors ($100K+ commitment) receive pro rata rights in subsequent rounds through Series A.
Founder VestingFounder shares subject to 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, monthly thereafter. Double-trigger acceleration on change of control.
Right of First RefusalCompany + Major Investors have ROFR on secondary sales of preferred or common.
Legal FeesCompany pays Investor legal fees up to $15,000 at close.
Closing ConditionsStandard: satisfactory DD · IP assignments in place · updated cap table · investor rights + voting agreements · founder employment agreements.
Target CloseSeptember 30, 2026 (30 days from signing this term sheet, subject to DD).

Exclusivity & No-Shop

No-Shop PeriodCompany agrees to 30 days of exclusivity following signature. During this window, Company will not solicit competing offers from other institutional investors.
Break-Up FeeIf Company terminates in bad faith, reimburses Investor DD costs up to $10K.
NON-BINDING: This term sheet is a summary of proposed terms only and does not constitute a legally binding agreement, except for the No-Shop and Confidentiality provisions above. Any binding agreement is subject to (i) satisfactory completion of due diligence, (ii) execution of definitive agreements (Stock Purchase Agreement, Investor Rights Agreement, Voting Agreement, ROFR/Co-Sale Agreement), and (iii) Board and shareholder approvals.
