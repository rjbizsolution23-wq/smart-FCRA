Key Clauses

Clause 1 · Cliff

One-Year Cliff

No shares vest during the first 12 months. On the first anniversary of vesting start (Jan 15, 2027), 25% of total shares (1,640,000) vest in a single event.

Clause 2 · Monthly Thereafter

Monthly Vesting Post-Cliff

After the cliff, remaining 75% (4,920,000 shares) vest in 36 equal monthly installments of 136,667 shares each, on the 15th of every month.

Clause 3 · Repurchase Right

Company Repurchase on Termination

If Founder is terminated (voluntary or for cause) before full vest, Company has 90-day option to repurchase all unvested shares at original issuance price ($0.001/share).

Clause 4 · Double-Trigger Acceleration

Change of Control Acceleration

Upon a Change of Control (acquisition, merger, IPO) followed by involuntary termination without cause within 12 months, 100% of unvested shares immediately accelerate to fully vested.

Clause 5 · Section 83(b) Election

Tax Election Filed

Founder confirms filing of Section 83(b) election with the IRS within 30 days of grant. Election on file · Reference № 83B-2026-RJ. Copy attached as Exhibit A.

Clause 6 · Transfer Restrictions

No Transfers Before Vest

Unvested shares are non-transferable. Vested shares subject to Company + Investor ROFR under Investor Rights Agreement.
