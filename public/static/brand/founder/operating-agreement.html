Article Summary · 12 Articles Referenced

Article I

Formation & Purpose

Formation under NM Revised Statutes Chapter 53 Article 19. Purpose: any lawful business, primarily AI-powered automation, credit technology, and business systems consulting.

Article II

Members & Capital Contributions

Initial capital contribution from Rick Jefferson: $10,000. Additional contributions optional; no member obligated beyond initial commitment.

Article III

Membership Interests & Voting

Membership interests represented as percentage ownership. Voting on major decisions requires majority (or supermajority for enumerated items).

Article IV

Management & Officers

Member-managed. Rick Jefferson serves as Managing Member with day-to-day authority. Officers may be appointed as company grows.

Article V

Allocations & Distributions

Profits/losses allocated in proportion to ownership. Distributions at discretion of Managing Member, subject to tax distribution requirements.

Article VI

Transfers & Assignments

Membership interests non-transferable without written consent. Right of first refusal to Company then remaining Members.

Article VII

Books & Records

Company maintains accounting records at principal office. Members may inspect on reasonable notice. Annual K-1 provided by March 15.

Article VIII

Dissolution

Dissolution on unanimous member consent, judicial decree, or specified triggering events. Assets distributed per NM statute + this Agreement.

Article IX

Indemnification

Company indemnifies Members and Officers to fullest extent permitted by NM law for actions taken in good faith on behalf of Company.

Article X

Amendment

Amendments require written consent of Members holding majority interest (supermajority for enumerated protective provisions).

Article XI

Dispute Resolution

Disputes resolved by binding arbitration in Bernalillo County, NM, under AAA Commercial Rules. Prevailing party recovers reasonable fees.

Article XII

Miscellaneous

Entire Agreement clause · Severability · Governing law: New Mexico · Notice provisions · Successors and assigns.
