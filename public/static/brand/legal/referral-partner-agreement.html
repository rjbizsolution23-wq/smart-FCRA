This Agreement governs a non-exclusive referral relationship between RJ Business Solutions ("Company") and the Referral Partner ("Partner"). 4. Terms & Conditions

Clause 4.1 · Attribution

Referral tracking window

A referral is considered attributed to Partner if a prospect (a) uses Partner's unique referral code at signup, or (b) mentions Partner by name in the discovery call and Company confirms the source within 90 days of first contact. Attribution disputes are resolved by written confirmation from the prospect.

Clause 4.2 · Payment

Commission payout schedule

Commission is paid Net-15 after Company collects payment from the referred client. Recurring subscription commissions are paid monthly on the collection anniversary. Payments made via ACH or Stripe. Minimum payout: $100 — smaller amounts roll to next cycle.

Clause 4.3 · Tier Advancement

Moving between tiers

Tier is evaluated on a rolling 12-month basis. Partners advancing to a new tier receive the new commission rate on the very next referral. Tier promotions are not retroactive to prior referrals unless negotiated in writing.

Clause 4.4 · Marketing Materials

Approved use of RJ brand

Partner may use RJ Business Solutions' logo, tagline, and marketing copy as provided in the Partner Media Kit. Partner may not modify the logo, misrepresent RJ services, or make claims Company has not authorized. Any independent marketing must be pre-approved via email.

Clause 4.5 · Non-Exclusivity

Both parties are free to work with others

This Agreement is non-exclusive for both parties. Partner may refer to competing services; Company may work with competing referral sources. Neither party owes the other a right of first refusal on any opportunity.

Clause 4.6 · Confidentiality

Client & company information

Partner agrees to hold in strict confidence all client information, pricing details, and internal Company operations shared during the course of this Agreement. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for three (3) years.

Clause 4.7 · Termination

How this ends

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. Commissions earned prior to termination continue to be paid through their full 12-month recurring window. Termination for cause (breach, fraud, misrepresentation) is immediate and voids future commissions.

5. General Provisions

Clause 5.1 · Governing Law

New Mexico, USA

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Mexico. Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Bernalillo County, NM, under AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules.

Clause 5.2 · Entire Agreement

Supersedes prior discussions

This Agreement, together with any signed addenda, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior verbal or written communications on the referral relationship.

Clause 5.3 · Amendments

Must be in writing

Any changes to commission rates, tiers, or terms must be agreed in writing (email is sufficient) and signed or acknowledged by both Parties before taking effect.
