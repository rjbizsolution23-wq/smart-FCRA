1. Purpose

The Parties intend to explore a potential business relationship (the "Purpose") and, in doing so, may exchange confidential information. This Agreement governs that exchange.

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"), whether oral, written, or digital — including business plans, client data, financial information, source code, system architectures, proprietary workflows, credit-adjacent data, pricing, and any material marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

3. Obligations of Receiving Party

Receiving Party shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose to any third party without prior written consent; (c) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (d) apply protections no less rigorous than those used for its own comparable information (minimum: reasonable care).

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was known prior to disclosure without confidentiality obligation; (c) is independently developed without reference to Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, provided prompt notice is given to the Disclosing Party.

5. Term & Return

Obligations survive for three (3) years from the Effective Date. Trade secrets remain protected indefinitely. Upon written request, Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within 15 business days.

6. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Mexico. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Bernalillo County, NM. This Agreement is the entire understanding of the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior communications on this subject.
