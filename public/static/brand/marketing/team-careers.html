● Operating Values

How we run.

The four rules that shape every decision we make.

01

Ship > Perfect

Momentum compounds. 7-day launches are our default. We iterate live, not in Figma.

02

Systems, not slogans

Every offer ships as infrastructure. If it can't be measured or automated, we don't sell it.

03

Compliance-first

Automation without guardrails is a lawsuit. FCRA, FDCPA, TCPA built in from day one.

04

Write it down

If it's not in a doc, it didn't happen. Memory doesn't scale. Documentation does.
