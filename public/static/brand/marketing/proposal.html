Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the growth system RJ Business Solutions will build for [Client Company] — a connected infrastructure that captures leads, automates follow-up, and turns conversations into booked revenue.

Our approach replaces scattered manual work with a working system: content and DMs flow into a CRM, AI-powered follow-up qualifies and routes leads, booking sync eliminates no-shows, and monthly reporting keeps the whole engine measurable.

The engagement below covers launch setup, monthly management, compliance guardrails, and the reporting infrastructure needed to track ROI from day one.

Scope of Work

1
Week 1

Discovery + System Design

Audit existing tools, define brand voice, map current lead flow, design the target architecture. Deliverable: system diagram + brand voice document.

2
Week 2

CRM + Pipeline Buildout

Configure CRM, build pipeline stages, wire booking calendar, integrate payment portal. Deliverable: functional CRM + booking flow.

3
Week 3

AI DM + Follow-Up Automation

Train AI on brand voice, build reply templates, wire escalation rules, deploy follow-up sequences and no-show recovery. Deliverable: live DM system + follow-up engine.

4
Week 4 & Ongoing

Launch + Monthly Management

Go live, monitor performance, monthly reporting, ongoing content system, human escalation handling, iterative optimization. Deliverable: launched system + monthly ops.
