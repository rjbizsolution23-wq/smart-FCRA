● About

Building the infrastructure behind generational wealth.

Rick Jefferson is a credit technology architect, AI systems builder, and business automation strategist. He operates at the intersection of credit expertise, fintech infrastructure, enterprise software, and executable business growth.

Through RJ Business Solutions, Rick builds the systems behind modern growth — AI-powered credit repair platforms, credit monitoring APIs, automated client communication, payment and subscription infrastructure, client portals, social media automation, DM follow-up systems, CRM workflows, and white-label fintech platforms.

His work centers on one belief: growth isn't more noise — growth is better infrastructure. Every offer RJ ships comes with the system to run it. Every founder Rick works with leaves the inbox for good.

Based in Tijeras, New Mexico. Speaker, podcast host of The Systems Brief, and hands-on operator across every RJ engagement.
