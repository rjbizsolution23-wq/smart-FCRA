Built for compliance

Designed so your portal does not invent outcomes.

Smart FCRA is software for credit repair organizations and advocacy teams. It does not guarantee deletions, score lifts, lending approval, or funding.

  • Evidence-first disputes — statements must come from the file or the consumer’s attestation.
  • CROA cancellation in-portal — clients can cancel without hunting through support tickets.
  • Completion before charge — covered credit-repair billing is gated on recorded service completion.
  • Named score models — VantageScore / FICO labeled when known; never a mystery “score.”
