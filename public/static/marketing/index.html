Violations & litigation

Built around the finding — not a blank form.

Every letter Smart FCRA generates is driven by what the engine found on that client’s report: statute, evidence, damages range, and which defendant gets the demand. Operators QA findings before anything mails.

  • Detect
    15-category violation engine

    FCRA accuracy and investigation failures, FDCPA collection abuses, ECOA discrimination flags, Metro 2 field defects, state overlays, and bankruptcy reporting issues — each tied to statute text and the account evidence on file.

  • Score
    Litigation Vulnerability Score + damages

    LVS ranks how trial-ready a finding is. Statutory and actual damage bands, willfulness framing, and attorney-fee estimates sit on the violation record so staff know what is worth fighting versus educating.

  • Generate
    Letters generated from the file — never fill-in templates

    Bureau § 611 disputes, furnisher § 623 challenges, method-of-verification demands, cease-and-desist, intent-to-sue, CFPB/AG paths, and specialty rounds are composed from the selected violations, account facts, and letter strategy — then branded as PDF under your org letterhead.

  • Cite
    Case law + SOL on the finding

    Findings carry case-law hooks and a statute-of-limitations calculator by state and debt type so operators do not chase time-barred accounts or invent citations.

  • Mail
    Click2Mail + FCRA § 611 clocks

    Approved letters can mail through Click2Mail. Each send starts the 30-day statutory investigation clock (35-day operational buffer) so the portal knows when a bureau is late.

Client portal & learning

What the consumer actually sees and does.

This is not a staff-only scanner. Clients work inside Smart FCRA: read the real report, confirm facts, approve disputes, learn their rights, and cancel under CROA without calling support.

Report sandbox

Scrollable paper copy of the imported Experian / Equifax / TransUnion file in a scriptless iframe — accounts, payment-history legend, hard vs soft inquiries, original PDF when vaulted. Viewing is not a dispute.

Evidence-first disputes

Structured fact interview and attestation. Identity-theft claims stay gated. The hallucination firewall blocks fabricated reasons and guaranteed-deletion language before a letter ever drafts.

Credit case & action plan

Findings as observations until staff QA, next-best-action for the consumer, credit-event ledger (deleted vs corrected vs balance change), and utilization education without fake FICO promises.

Learning resources

Consumer Rights hub (FCRA / CROA / TSR / FDCPA / identity theft), Credit Tutor (Alex Rivera), strategy mentors for staff, journey rituals, and document vault for every generated letter the client is allowed to see.

CROA / TSR billing controls

In-portal cancel services. Analysis unlock blocked until service completion is recorded on the ledger. Named score models only — VantageScore / FICO labeled when known.

Ops that stay attached

Staff console for clients, violation QA, letter generation, mailing, MFSN imports, GHL sync, tradelines, fundability, and RON legal sessions — same RJ Business Solutions brand the client already trusts.

Compliance posture

Software for operators — not outcome guarantees.

Smart FCRA does not promise deletions, score lifts, lending approval, or funding. It documents the file, the finding, the letter, and the clock.

  • Facts before fight — dispute language must come from the report or the consumer’s attestation.
  • QA before mail — staff review findings; Metro 2 cross-bureau variance stays REVIEW/OBSERVATION until someone owns it.
  • CROA cancellation in-portal — not buried in a support ticket.
  • Completion before charge — covered credit-repair billing waits on a recorded service completion.
