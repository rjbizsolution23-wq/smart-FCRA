# Strategy — FCRA Supreme Violation Detector

## Market Position
The FCRA Supreme Violation Detector is positioned as the elite, enterprise-grade analysis engine for credit repair professionals and law firms specializing in FCRA/FDCPA litigation. Unlike simple credit monitoring apps, this tool provides high-precision violation detection with statutory damage calculations.

## Monetization Model (Stripe-Powered — No Free Trials)
SmartFCRA™ Supreme operates on a strict subscription-only model with zero free trials to preserve system integrity and attorney-level operational performance:
- **Basic Plan ($497/mo)**: Mapped to 'professional' internally. Up to 100 active clients, 100 deep-scan analyses/mo, 15 high-fidelity FCRA/FDCPA dispute templates, automated response tracking, litigation scoring (v2.4), and priority email support.
- **Unlimited Plan ($2500/mo)**: Mapped to 'unlimited' internally. Unlimited clients/scans, multi-bureau MFSN & SmartCredit imports, USPS Certified Mail Click2Mail API integration, custom letter builder, up to 20 team seats, and 4-hour SLA support.
- **Enterprise Plan ($9997/mo)**: Mapped to 'enterprise' internally. Unlimited everything (users, clients, scans, API mailings), 38 pleadings/motions templates, 300+ landmarks case law database, white-label portals, REST API access, and direct legal ops consultations with Rick Jefferson.

## Competitive Advantages
1. **Speed**: Sub-second analysis of 3-Bureau MFSN reports at the Edge.
2. **Precision**: Statutorily backed violation detection (15 U.S.C. § 1681).
3. **Monetization**: Built-in Stripe billing with organization-level quotas.
4. **Resilience**: Deployed on Cloudflare Workers/D1 for zero-downtime performance.

## Growth Strategy
1. **Targeting Professionals**: B2B focus on credit repair organizations (CROs).
2. **API First**: Allowing third-party integrations for high-volume processors.
3. **Litigation Focused**: Positioning the tool as the starting point for legal action, increasing the "value per analysis".
