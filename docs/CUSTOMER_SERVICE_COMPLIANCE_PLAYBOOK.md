# Smart FCRA — Customer Service, Call Center & CRM Compliance Playbook

**Document Owner:** Compliance / Customer Operations  
**Applies To:** Customer Service, Sales Support, Onboarding, Billing, Technical Support, Dispute Operations, Supervisors, QA, Compliance and Contractors  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Every Smart FCRA representative must provide accurate, professional and properly documented customer service **without**:

- Guaranteeing credit-score increases, deletions, results, lawsuit outcomes, or monetary damages
- Misrepresenting legal rights or representing Smart FCRA analysis as a court determination
- Giving unauthorized legal advice or encouraging false disputes or false identity-theft claims
- Altering consumer-supplied facts or misrepresenting bureau/furnisher/collector communications
- Concealing pricing/contract terms or obstructing legitimate cancellation requests

Representatives must distinguish: **customer facts**, **record information**, **Smart FCRA-generated analysis**, and **legal conclusions requiring qualified counsel**.

## 2. The Golden Rule

**NEVER PROMISE AN OUTCOME.**

May explain: what Smart FCRA does, what the system identified, workflow status, documents, correspondence, deadlines, responses, contractual services, platform actions.

May **not** promise deletions, score increases, guaranteed violations, guaranteed recovery, lawsuit wins, or coaching false statements.

## 3–5. Call Opening & Identity Verification

**Opening:** "Thank you for calling Smart FCRA. My name is [AGENT NAME]. May I have your name and the email address or other approved identifier associated with your account?"

Verify identity before disclosing protected information. Never disclose reports, PII, findings, or correspondence before authentication.

## 6–10. Core Objection Handling

| Customer asks | Approved response (summary) |
|---|---|
| Fix my credit? | Tools to identify/document issues; results vary; no deletion/score guarantees |
| Score increase? | Cannot predict or guarantee score change |
| How long? | Depends on process/responses; explain documented milestone, not guaranteed date |
| Is this a violation? | Finding may warrant review; not same as court determination; escalate legal opinions |
| How much money? | Analytical estimates only; not guaranteed liability or recovery |

## 11–13. Factual Integrity

- Never change consumer statements (e.g. "balance wrong" → "not mine")
- Identity theft: truthful workflow only; never suggest false ID theft strategy
- Never invent facts for the consumer

## 14–18. Findings, Deletions, Responses

Explain findings as **potential issues** with evidence chain: Finding → Evidence → Framework → Action.

On deletions: note report change; do not promise score lift or permanence.

On reinsertion: compare reports and correspondence; do not immediately claim illegal reinsertion.

Document all bureau/furnisher/collector responses with sender, date, account, type, changes, next review.

## 19–27. Escalations & Special Callers

- Attorneys/regulators/media: route to authorized team; no substantive statements without authorization
- Refunds: document request; follow billing workflow; never auto-deny or auto-promise
- Cancellation: treat any clear request as cancellation; do not hide controls or require magic words
- Chargeback/regulator threats: document; never threaten or retaliate

## 28–37. Security & Privacy

- No passwords, full SSNs in notes, or PCI data in CRM text
- Enhanced verification for email/phone/MFA/payment changes
- Privacy requests: use approved workflow with tracked deadlines

## 38–40. Human-in-the-Loop

Flag AI errors for review before reliance. High-impact outputs (findings, damages scenarios, identity theft, correspondence) require appropriate human review.

CRM notes: **FACTS → ACTION → RESULT → NEXT STEP** — professional, factual, never pejorative.

## 41–43. Audit & Dispositions

Maintain tamper-evident history. Use standardized dispositions (General Inquiry, Finding Explanation, Billing, Refund, Cancellation, Complaint, Privacy Request, AI Review, etc.).

Escalation levels 1–6: CS → Senior → Operations → Compliance → Legal → Security/Privacy.

## 44–46. Red Flags & QA

Red-flag terms (lawyer, sue, CFPB, chargeback, cancel, guarantee, etc.) trigger review — not proof of wrongdoing.

QA scorecard weights: Authentication 15%, Privacy 15%, Accuracy 15%, Compliance 20%, CX 10%, Resolution 10%, Documentation 10%, Escalation 5%.

Critical failures: unauthorized disclosure, password requests, false dispute coaching, guaranteeing recovery, obstructing cancellation, falsifying notes.

## 47–52. CRM Compliance Objects

Structured records: Consumer, Consent, Contract, Service, Payment, Call/SMS/Email, Ticket, Complaint, Cancellation, Refund, Report, Finding, Evidence, Dispute, Correspondence, Mailing, Deadline, Response, AI Output, Human Review, Escalation, Privacy Request, Security Incident, Audit Event.

Finding object must store evidence chain — never store only "FCRA violation."

## 53–57. Closing Standards

End-of-call summary: issue, action, status, responsibilities, next step.

**Service Standard:** Never fabricate. Never guarantee. Never hide. Never pressure. Never alter evidence. Never obstruct cancellation. Document. Preserve evidence. Escalate beyond authority.
